English

What the link looks like

  • It starts with square.link: Square processes the payment.
  • It shows your project and company name, and the amount you approved in the proposal.
  • Square emails you the receipt.

We never ask for card or bank details by email, text or phone. If anything doesn't match your proposal, don't pay and email us.
