Our guarantee

If we don't do everything that's on us (the full list with the spec paragraph for each item, every request and reminder on time, and the package organized with what came in plus the pending list), you don't pay the balance. If something is missing because a third party didn't send it, the balance is still due.

If we see within 2 business days of getting your documents that we can't help, we refund the deposit.

Payment goes through Square: the link starts with square.link, shows your project and the approved amount, and Square emails you the receipt. We never ask for card or bank details by email, text or phone.

What we don't do

  • Signatures or seals
  • Field work or drawing as-builts
