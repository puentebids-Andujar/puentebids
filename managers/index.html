How it works

You keep paying the crew for the work, like any vendor. We never charge the property manager.

  1. Send the list

    Property, units, scope and what you pay per unit or per job.

  2. An insured crew confirms

    The crew agrees to your price and start date. You get their COI and W-9 before day one.

  3. Work and papers

    A lien waiver with each payment, and one contact for the crew and the paperwork.

The papers, handled

Chasing a crew's insurance and tax forms takes time your team doesn't have. We keep them current.

COI

Certificate of insurance sent before the crew starts. If your agreement needs your company as additional insured, we ask the crew's agent for it.

W-9

On file before the first invoice. The crew sends it directly; we make sure it arrives.

Lien waivers

One with every payment, so nothing is left open on your property.
